Our Story

From farm to table, a journey of passion and flavor

Where It All Began

The Rustic Spoon was born in 2015 from a simple dream: to create a dining experience that celebrates the connection between the land and the table. Our founder, Chef Marcus Thompson, grew up on a family farm where he learned that the best meals start with the freshest ingredients.

After training in some of the world's finest kitchens, Marcus returned home with a vision to bring that farm-to-table philosophy to our community. He partnered with local farmers, artisans, and producers who share his commitment to quality and sustainability.

Today, we continue that tradition by sourcing 80% of our ingredients from within 50 miles of our kitchen. Every dish tells a story of the seasons, the soil, and the dedicated hands that nurture our food from seed to plate.
